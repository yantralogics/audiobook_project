# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## Project Overview

Create a charming, engaging audiobook experience for a 4-year-old daughter. The audiobook should feel warm, safe, and easy to follow, with simple narration, playful characters, and gentle pacing.

## Goals

- Deliver a short, high-quality audiobook suitable for a preschool audience.
- Encourage imagination, emotional connection, and language development.
- Provide a convenient format for parents to play during bedtime, car rides, or quiet time.
- Make the audiobook easy to update, extend, and reuse for future stories.
- Start with a 30-second synopsis that shows the high-level narration, story arc, and audio/narration style before creating the full story.

## Target Audience

- Primary: Meera, a bright-eyed, happy, goofy, adventurous 4-year-old girl
- Secondary: parents and caregivers who will play the audiobook for Meera

## Key Features

### 1. Story Content

- A single cohesive story with a clear beginning, middle, and end.
- Simple vocabulary and short, child-friendly sentences.
- Positive themes like friendship, curiosity, kindness, adventure, natural elements, discovery, bravery, and problem solving.
- Age-appropriate conflict and resolution.
- A relatable main character and at least one gentle supporting character.
- Personalize the story with Meera’s favorite activities and companions: coloring, playing doctor with stuffies, cats, unicorns, and fun friends.

### 2. Narration Style

- Warm, calm, and expressive narration.
- Pacing that allows the child to absorb the story without rushing.
- Occasional simple sound cues or pauses to support comprehension.

### 3. Audio Production

- Clean recording with low background noise.
- Soft music or sound effects only if they support the mood and do not distract.
- Preferably one narrator voice to maintain consistency.

### 4. Format and Delivery

- Export audio as a standard file format (MP3 or AAC).
- Provide a companion script or transcript in markdown for future editing.
- Define one or more listening sessions of 8-12 minutes each.

## Requirements

### Functional Requirements

- FR1: The audiobook must contain at least one complete story.
- FR2: The story should be no more than 12 minutes long when narrated.
- FR3: A transcript must be included in the project files.
- FR4: The final audio file must be playable on common devices (phone, tablet, smart speaker).
- FR5: The story should include at least one positive moral or lesson.
- FR6: A 30-second synopsis must be created first, describing the high-level narration and reflecting the intended audio and narration style.

### Non-functional Requirements

- NFR1: Audio quality should be clean and easy to understand.
- NFR2: Content should be appropriate for a 4-year-old audience.
- NFR3: The tone should feel nurturing and gentle.
- NFR4: The audiobook should be easy to update for future stories.

## Success Metrics

- Story length remains within the target span.
- Review from caregiver indicates the story is engaging and soothing.
- Child remains attentive through the majority of the audiobook.
- Audio playback is smooth and accessible on standard devices.

## Scope and Out of Scope

### In Scope

- Writing and producing a new bedtime-style story.
- Creating audio narration and a transcript.
- Documenting story structure and production notes.

### Out of Scope

- Developing a mobile or web app.
- Interactive story elements.
- Full series production beyond the initial story.

## Milestones

- 30-second synopsis created and reviewed.
- Story draft completed.
- Narration and recording done.
- Audio edited and reviewed.
- Final file exported and verified.

## Notes

- Consider incorporating the daughter’s favorite animals, activities, or names for personalization.
- Keep the tone uplifting and reassuring.
- Prefer a story length that fits a bedtime or quiet-time routine.
